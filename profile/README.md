# Koyomi

**Free, open-source health and fitness apps that work offline and keep your data on your phone.**

Koyomi (暦) is the Japanese word for calendar: a record of your days. Each app here helps you keep a log of your own health, and keeps that log on your device.

## Apps

### Kitae (鍛え): workout tracker

Plan your training days, log your sets and watch your lifts progress. Kitae works fully offline and has no tracking. The name is Japanese for training, in the sense of tempering something until it is strong.

- **Android:** [source](https://github.com/gureetk/kitae) · F-Droid Coming Soon
- **iPhone:** [source](https://github.com/gureetk/kitae-ios) · [Testflight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/GTKbw4Hg)

Backups are plain zip files, and a backup made on one platform restores on the other.

## How we build

- **Offline first.** Your data stays on your phone. Backups are files you keep wherever you like.
- **Open source.** Every app is free software, and each repository states its own licence.
- **Private by design.** No tracking and no ads.
